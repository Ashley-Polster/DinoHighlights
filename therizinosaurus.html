Therizinosaurus

Overview

Diet: herbivorous

Length: 31.5 ft

Map

Image found on PLOS by Khishigjav Tsogtbaatar, David B. Weishampel, David C. Evans, Mahito Watabe under CC BY 2.5

Location: Mongolia

Time period: Late Cretaceous (70 million years ago)

Fun Facts

  • Therizinosaurus means "scythe lizard"
  • One of the few herbivorous theropods
  • 50 cm long claws
