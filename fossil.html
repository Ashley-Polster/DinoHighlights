What is a Fossil?

Fossils are physical evidence of prehistoric animals and plants. They tell us about the history of our planet, from climate and evolution to diets and diseases.

Types of Fossils

There are three major categories of fossils: body, trace, and chemical

Body Fossils

Preserved parts of the physical body of an organism.

Trace Fossils

Evidence of organism activity, rather than the body itself.

Chemical Fossils

Molecular evidence or chemicals found in rocks that indicate past biological activity, such as oil, coal, and gas.

Timeline of Fossilization Process

1. Death

An organism dies and its remains settle on the ground or water floor.

2. Burial

Sediment quickly covers the remains, protecting them from decay.

3. Compression

Layers of sediment build up, applying pressure over time.

4. Mineralization

Minerals replace organic material, turning it into rock.

5. Erosion & Discovery

Weathering exposes the fossil, allowing it to be discovered.
